What is "severe service"? (Chicago winters & stop‑and‑go)

  • Frequent short trips: Engine doesn't reach full operating temperature, causing moisture buildup and oil contamination.
  • Extreme cold: Chicago winters thicken oil, stress batteries, and increase wear on starting components.
  • Stop‑and‑go traffic: Idling and constant braking generate heat, wearing brakes, transmission, and cooling system faster.
  • Towing or hauling: Adds strain to engine, transmission, and suspension.
  • Dusty or salted roads: Winter road salt accelerates corrosion; dust clogs air filters.

If any of these apply (and for most Chicagoland drivers, they do), your vehicle is operating under "severe service" conditions. That means you should follow the severe maintenance schedule in your manual—often requiring more frequent oil changes, fluid checks, and inspections.
